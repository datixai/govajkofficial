# Brief

- **Project:** redesign of ajk.gov.pk, to pitch to the Government of AJ&K. Local test first.
- **Reference:** https://ajk.gov.pk/ (the client's own site).
- **Keep:** all photos, tabs (menu items), news, departments, helplines and details from the current site.
- **Change:** a modern, expert-level home page design; fix all issues and incorrect information; finish the parts that were not developed; remove the flag video; chinar leaf favicon.
- **Identity:** AJ&K as the base camp of the freedom struggle for Occupied Jammu & Kashmir; the identity and history of Jammu & Kashmir must be visible.
- **Add:** an admin panel with full control (add, edit, upload, delete any content); a better YouTube video system.
- **Security:** top level, as for a government site that may be attacked.
- **Tech:** latest Next.js (16.4.2).
